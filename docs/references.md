# Acknowledgements and references

This project builds on robosuite and MuJoCo for simulation, PyAV/FFmpeg and
OpenCV for video processing, NumPy/SciPy for numerical work, and Matplotlib for
figures. Thanks to the maintainers of those projects and of uv, pytest, Ruff,
ImageIO and PyYAML.
